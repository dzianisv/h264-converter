Privacy

Convert264 has no account, no ads, and no tracking. Conversion runs entirely on your phone — the app does not even have internet permission, so your videos cannot leave your device. Full text: Privacy Policy.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Free, no ads, no in-app purchases, no watermark. The source code is on GitHub.

Does it upload my videos anywhere?

No. Everything happens on your phone. The app has no internet permission at all.

Which formats can it convert to?

MP4, MKV, MOV, and WebM, with H.264, H.265, VP9, or MPEG-4 video and AAC, MP3, Opus, Vorbis, or FLAC audio — or copy the streams without re-encoding.

Why is the download 55 MB?

Most of it is the FFmpeg engine that does the actual converting, built into the app so nothing is uploaded to a server.

Is it on Google Play yet?

Not yet — the Play listing is being prepared. Until then, install the APK from this page. Each release is signed with a fresh key, so uninstall the older version first.

Something does not work. Where do I report it?

Open an issue on GitHub — every report gets an answer: github.com/dzianisv/h264-converter/issues.
