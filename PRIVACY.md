# Privacy Policy — Convert264

Last updated: November 12, 2026.

Convert264 is an offline video converter. This policy explains what the app
does — and does not — collect.

## What the app collects

Nothing. Convert264 has no account system, no analytics SDK, no advertising
SDK, and no tracking. It does not collect, store, or transmit personal data.

## What happens to your videos

Conversion runs entirely on your device. Your videos are never uploaded,
shared, or sent anywhere. Converted files are saved to your device's storage
and nowhere else.

## Permissions

- Storage / media access: to read the videos you choose and save the
  converted files. The app reads only the files you pick or share to it.
- No internet permission: the app cannot access the network.

## Third parties

The app uses Google Play services only for the optional in-app rating prompt
(after you have converted several videos). That prompt is provided by Google
Play; any data it collects is governed by Google's privacy policy, not by us.

If the app is downloaded from Google Play, Google Play itself collects
installation and crash statistics (Android Vitals). That data is visible to
the developer in aggregate form in Play Console and is governed by Google's
privacy policy.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here.
