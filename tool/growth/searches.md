Keywords from the store listing (tool/play-store/listing.md). Scored Nov 14,
2026 (WL-11, pulled forward from Nov 20).

## Method

- Relevance 1–5: how directly the term matches what the app actually does
  (5 = the core job, in the title/short description).
- Difficulty 1–5: grounded in the WL-07 competitor file (Nov 14, live Play
  listings) — 5 = a 5M+ install leader with 80K–182K ratings owns the term,
  1 = no top-5 competitor pitches it at all.
- Volume High/Med/Low: QUALITATIVE estimate only, from category demand
  evidence (leader install tiers, review counts). Not measured — no keyword
  tool or Play Console data exists yet. Re-score from Play Console search
  terms once the listing is live.
- Priority: Quick win = relevance 5 + difficulty ≤3; Core fight = relevance
  5 + difficulty 4–5 (win via listing + rating, not first); Avoid = low
  relevance whatever the volume.
