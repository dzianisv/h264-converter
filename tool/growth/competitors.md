# Convert264 — competitors

Researched Nov 13, 2026 (WL-07, first pass — ratings, 1-star complaints,
update cadence from Play/AppBrain search results; screenshots not yet
checked from the live listings). Sources: Google Play pages + AppBrain,
all read Nov 13, 2026. No numbers invented.

## 1. Compress Video Size Compressor — Inverse AI

- Package: `com.video_converter.video_compressor`
- Rating: **4.5★, ~182K ratings** (182K header / 178K phone breakdown, Nov 14, 2026; 1★ 18,425 of 178K)
- Updated: Oct 27, 2026 (frequent — updated within the last month)
- Downloads: 5M+
- NOTE (Nov 14, 2026): this package now shows as "Compress Video Size
  Compressor", NOT "Video Converter, Compressor" as the Nov 13 first pass
  recorded — same developer, same rating count and update date, so most
  likely a retitle of the same listing (or the first pass conflated two
  Inverse AI listings). One 1-star reviewer (Argent Zet, Oct 25) calls
  it a deliberately worsened version of the same developer's "Video
  Converter, Compressor".
- RESOLVED (Nov 15, 2026 re-check): the first pass conflated two distinct
  Inverse AI listings. `com.video_converter.video_compressor` IS
  "Compress Video Size Compressor" (Play, updated Oct 11, 2026; 4.5★,
  ~173–178K ratings); "Video Converter, Compressor" is the separate
  package `com.inverseai.video_converter` (AppBrain data Nov 7, 2026:
  18M installs, 4.5★, 209,288 ratings, last updated Jan 5, 2026) —
  the larger of the two, and the real category leader by ratings.
- Free with ads + PRO paywall; conversion to MP4/MKV/AVI/3GP/FLV/MTS/M4V/MOV
  is a PRO feature. Batch queue included.
- 1-star complaints (Nov 13): lifetime purchase advertised $9.99 but
  charged $16.53; conversion formats locked behind PRO; ads (the reason
  Convert264 exists — tried Nov 8, 2026, rejected for ads + awful UI/UX).
- The category leader by review count. Bar to beat: 4.5★ at 182K ratings.
- Screenshots (Nov 14, first three): 1) "No.1 Video Compressor" —
  Multiple Compress Options, Custom Settings, Batch Compression, Trim
  and Cut, Instant Preview; 2) "High-Quality Compression for Multiple
  Videos" — 278 MB → 19 MB, 720P/1080P/4K, multiple files at a time,
  358 MB → 23 MB (93% save); 3) "Compress Video With Multiple Profile"
  — Trim, Compression Speed Normal, Codec x264, Format Original,
  Small/Medium/Large profiles + COMPRESS button. The pitch is
  compression ratios and batch, not formats.
- Newest 1-star, top 10 (Nov 14): paywall/subscription loop dominates
  ("every single useful feature locked behind a subscription",
  "stuck in a loop, always asking to subscribe", must pay for Low/High
  quality, only Medium free), then ads (forced ad-watching for
  everything, ad spam), then failures ("always fails", "useless",
  over-compressed output, needs internet). Several non-English reviews
  (Tagalog, Persian, Vietnamese) — same themes.

## 2. Video Converter — VidSoftLab

- Package: `jaineel.videoconvertor`
- Rating: **4.5★, ~80K ratings** (83.4K header / 80.3K phone breakdown,
  Nov 14, 2026; 1★ 6,741 of 80.3K)
- Installs: 5M+ on Play; AppBrain: 8M total, ~20K in the last 30 days
- Updated: Nov 23, 2025 (a year stale — update cadence is slow)
- Size: 64 MB. Contains ads + in-app purchases. FFmpeg-based (LGPL).
- 1-star complaints: "Does its job (60% of the time)"; "ads are awful";
  free tier limited, quality slightly degraded; **no queue option**.
- 8M installs with a year-old build: the demand is durable, the product
  is coasting. Beating it = same formats, no ads, a real queue, current build.
- Screenshots (Nov 14, first three): 1) Wide banner "Video Converter —
  Your Freedom To Creativity" over a Welcome screen (Video Tools: Video
  Cutter, Join Video, SlowMotion Video, Reverse Video, Compress Video;
  Audio Tools: Audio Converter, Video to Audio, Cut Audio, Join Audio)
  plus a long format list (MP4, AVI, MOV, MKV, WEBM, WMV, F4V, FLV,
  M4V, MPEG, MPG, GP3, ASF, M2TS, MTS, TS, VOB, OGV); 2) same Welcome
  screen, "Convert Video/Audio to Any Format & Any Gadget"; 3) close-up
  of the Welcome screen, no headline. The pitch is breadth: many tools,
  many formats — no quality or speed claim.
- Newest 1-star, top 10 (Nov 14): "never retains the videos' quality",
  "doesn't convert videos like it says", wrong vertical-video scaling,
  "converted only 15 MB out of 1.4 GB avi" — reliability/quality, not
  ads, leads the newest complaints. Several 1-star reviews carry
  positive or gibberish text ("good 👍", "super 👍👍", "ngubk jhb").
  Older most-relevant 1-star: very slow conversion (700 MB took
  2 hours), ads too frequent, second conversion blocked unless
  Premium is bought.

## 5. FFmpeg Media Encoder — SilentLexx

- Package: `com.silentlexx.ffmpeggui`
- Rating: **3.7★ header / 3.8★ phone breakdown, ~4.5K ratings** (4.48K
  header / 4.16K phone breakdown, Nov 14, 2026; 1★ 994 of 4.16K —
  ~24% 1-star, the highest share of the five)
- Updated: Aug 28, 2026 (Play) — cadence roughly yearly
- Downloads: 500K+
- Free, exposes raw FFmpeg flags. Power users only.
- 1-star complaints: output video doesn't appear / appears hours later;
  going to background re-encodes the same file simultaneously, leaving
  corrupt files + dead battery; crashes when selecting the output folder;
  hard to navigate, not beginner-friendly. A video over 4 min gets
  auto-reconverted ~3 min after finishing (bug since the FFmpeg 6.0 update).
- The cautionary tale for Convert264: this is what a raw FFmpeg wrapper
  looks like when lifecycle bugs ship. Our WL-12 bug (0-byte file reported
  Done) is the same class — fix it before launch, not after.
- Screenshots (Nov 14): raw dark terminal-style UI, no marketing
  headlines — 1) main screen with Input/Output paths, Resolution
  1920x812, Template Audio M4A (aac), raw command
  "-vn -c:a aac -ab 128k -ar 44100", VIDEO/AUDIO CODEC buttons;
  2) batch file list with checkboxes, Progress 41%, Log button;
  3) "Converting: 8 from 15", log line with bitrate/speed, Progress
  90%. The screenshots sell nothing — they show the tool. Our
  screenshots must not look like this.
- Newest 1-star, top 10 (Nov 14): can't select/find files dominates
  (media selector shows ~0.2% of files, no "media file" permission
  on newer phones, MKV remux not recognized, whole folders no longer
  selectable), then no output file created ("goes through the whole
  conversion, no file created"), progress duplicating/overwriting the
  finished file, and "interface is terrible". Most-helpful 1-star
  (Dragoş Dumitriu, Jun 1, 2025): the app "used to be a great tool"
  but broke about a year earlier — "one can no longer select files
  to be converted, which renders the whole tool useless."

## Notable mention: Video Transcoder — Branden Archer (open source)

- Package: `protect.videoeditor`
- Rating: 3.4★, ~630 ratings. Last updated **Jan 2019** — abandoned.
- Open source, FFmpeg, no ads, no IAPs, no network permission. Reviewers
  call it "the Android App Golden Standard" and love it despite the age.
- This is Convert264's exact niche (private, free, no-ads FFmpeg converter)
  and it has had no maintained occupant since 2019. The gap is real.

## Repeated complaints across competitors (the demand)

Confirmed by the Nov 14 deeper sampling (top 10 newest 1-star reviews
per app, from the live Play listings) — the ranking below held:

1. **Ads everywhere** — the #1 complaint in every top app. Convert264's
   no-ads edge is the headline, not a footnote.
2. **Paywalled formats / surprise charges** — PRO locks on basic conversion,
   lifetime price ≠ charged price. Free means free.
3. **Reliability: outputs that never appear, corrupt files, crashes** —
   VidSoftLab "60% of the time", SilentLexx background re-encode,
   Inverse AI compressor crashes. A converter that silently produces a
   dead file is the worst failure in the category (see WL-12).
4. **No queue / no batch** — VidSoftLab has none; Inverse AI has one behind
   its compressor. Convert264's queue is a differentiator, not a commodity.
5. **Hard to use** — SilentLexx is powerful but "not beginner friendly".
   Presets + one-tap convert win over flag exposure.

## What this means for Convert264

- The bar: 4.5★ is table stakes; the leaders hold it with ads + paywalls +
  stale builds. A 4.5★+ no-ads, current, reliable converter has a real opening.
- The 1-star list is our feature list: no ads, no paywalled formats,
  queue + batch, outputs that always appear (WL-12), simple presets.
- Target to beat first: VidSoftLab (8M installs, stale since Nov 2025) on
  reliability + freshness; Inverse AI's "Video Converter, Compressor"
  (18M installs, ~209K ratings — the real category leader, see §1
  resolution Nov 15) on honesty (no ads, no surprise charges).
